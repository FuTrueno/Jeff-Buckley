Artist Profile

A voice that still feels close.

Jeff Buckley was an American singer, songwriter, and guitarist whose work combined intimate songwriting with an unusually expressive vocal style. His album Grace remains the central point of his recorded legacy.

The Grace Era

Released in 1994, Grace introduced Buckley's distinctive blend of alternative rock, folk, soul, and carefully arranged guitar work. The record includes songs such as “Last Goodbye,” “Grace,” and “Lover, You Should've Come Over.”
